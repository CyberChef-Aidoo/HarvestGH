Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a farmer? ▼
Go to Register as Farmer and fill in your details. You can choose to manage your own account or let HarvestGH manage it for you. Registration is completely free.
I am elderly and cannot use a phone well. Can I still join? ▼
Yes. When you register, choose "Manage it for me." HarvestGH will handle your listing and contact you directly by phone call when a buyer is found. You do not need to use any app or website after registering.
How quickly will I find a buyer? ▼
It depends on the crop, your region, and how many buyers are registered in your area. During peak harvest seasons, matches can be made within 24–48 hours. You will receive an SMS immediately when a match is found.
Is HarvestGH free to use? ▼
Registration and listing are completely free. HarvestGH earns a small transaction fee (1–2%) when a deal is successfully completed. There are no upfront costs for farmers or buyers.
I registered but have not heard anything. What should I do? ▼
Check your listing status at farmer-portal.html using your phone number. If your listing is active, we are still searching for a buyer. You can also call or WhatsApp us for an update.
Can I partner with HarvestGH as an NGO or development organization? ▼
Yes — we actively welcome partnerships with NGOs, government agencies, and development organizations. Send us a message using the contact form above, selecting "Partnership or collaboration" as the subject.
