Agrobridge Image Assets Specifications
========================================

The following image files are required by the Agrobridge frontend application.
Place the image files directly in public/images/ using the exact filenames listed below.

1. /images/hero.jpg
   - Purpose: Homepage hero section background photo.
   - Recommended Dimensions: 1920 x 1080 px (or 2560 x 1440 px), 16:9 ratio.
   - Content: Real farm or produce collection scene in Ghana's producing regions.
   - Format: Optimized JPEG (< 350 KB).

2. /images/cta.jpg
   - Purpose: Homepage bottom "Ready to buy or sell" banner background photo.
   - Recommended Dimensions: 1920 x 800 px (or 1600 x 600 px), ~2:1 panoramic ratio.
   - Content: Farmers aggregating produce or bags/crates ready for transport.
   - Format: Optimized JPEG (< 300 KB).

3. /images/shop-header.jpg
   - Purpose: Header background banner for Marketplace (/shop), About (/about), and Legal documents (/privacy, /terms).
   - Recommended Dimensions: 1920 x 600 px (or 1600 x 500 px), wide banner ratio.
   - Content: Fresh crop harvest, sorted crates or bags in natural light.
   - Format: Optimized JPEG (< 250 KB).

4. /images/farmland.jpg
   - Purpose: About page mission visual.
   - Recommended Dimensions: 1200 x 800 px, 3:2 ratio.
   - Content: Ghanaian farmland or farmers at work.
   - Format: Optimized JPEG (< 250 KB).

5. Crop and Poultry Product Photos (Catalogue):
   - /images/tomatoes.jpg (800 x 600 px, 4:3 ratio) - Crated fresh tomatoes
   - /images/maize.jpg (800 x 600 px, 4:3 ratio) - Dried white maize in 100kg bags
   - /images/mango.jpg (800 x 600 px, 4:3 ratio) - Harvested mangoes in boxes
   - /images/plantain.jpg (800 x 600 px, 4:3 ratio) - Bunches of green Apantu/Apem plantain
   - /images/eggs.jpg (800 x 600 px, 4:3 ratio) - Crates of fresh farm eggs (30 per crate)
   - /images/poultry.jpg (800 x 600 px, 4:3 ratio) - Live broilers or dressed whole chickens

6. Brand Mark:
   - /images/logo.jpeg
   - Purpose: Navigation brand mark and footer branding.
   - Recommended Dimensions: 512 x 512 px (or vector SVG).

Note: If a product image fails to load or is missing, ProductCard and ShopCard automatically render a solid brand-pale terracotta block with the crop name as a fallback instead of defaulting to a generic stock photo.
