💡 How Your Commission Works

Every time one of your registered farmers completes a deal through HarvestGH, you earn 1% of the deal value. This is paid from HarvestGH's platform fee — your farmers pay nothing extra.

Example: Your farmer Ama sells 80 bags of tomatoes at GH₵120/bag = GH₵9,600 deal. You earn GH₵96.

Commissions are paid to your phone via MoMo within 48 hours of each completed deal.
