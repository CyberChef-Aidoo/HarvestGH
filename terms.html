2. What HarvestGH Is

HarvestGH is a digital marketplace that connects smallholder farmers with buyers of agricultural produce in Ghana. Our platform allows:

  • Farmers to list available produce for sale
  • Buyers to register their interest in purchasing specific crops
  • HarvestGH to facilitate matches and notify both parties via SMS
  • Delivery coordination between matched parties

HarvestGH is a marketplace intermediary only. We are not a party to any transaction between farmers and buyers. We do not buy, sell, or take ownership of any agricultural produce at any time. All transactions are directly between farmers and buyers.

HarvestGH does not guarantee that any listing will result in a match, that any match will result in a completed transaction, or that produce will be of a specific quality, quantity, or condition.

9. Disputes Between Users

HarvestGH facilitates connections but is not a party to transactions. However, we are committed to helping resolve disputes fairly.

How to Raise a Dispute

  1. Contact HarvestGH within 24 hours of receiving goods (or of the agreed transaction date if goods were not delivered)
  2. Provide your phone number, the match reference, and a description of the issue
  3. HarvestGH will contact both parties and attempt to mediate within 48 hours

Common Dispute Types

  • Quantity shortfall: Farmer delivered less than listed. HarvestGH will mediate a price adjustment or partial refund.
  • Quality issue: Produce was not as described. HarvestGH will assess and mediate.
  • Non-delivery: Farmer did not fulfil the order. HarvestGH will attempt to source an alternative and may remove the farmer from the platform.
  • Non-payment: Buyer did not pay. HarvestGH will contact the buyer and may remove them from the platform.

HarvestGH's mediation decisions are made in good faith but are not legally binding. For legal disputes, parties are advised to seek resolution through Ghana's courts or the appropriate regulatory body.
