Our Mission

Connect Every Farm
to a Market

Ghana loses between 30–50% of its harvest every year to post-harvest waste. Not because farmers are unproductive — but because there is no reliable, fast way for a farmer in Brong-Ahafo to reach a buyer in Accra before tomatoes spoil.

HarvestGH is that bridge. We connect smallholder FBO farmers directly to verified bulk buyers — through SMS, the web, and WhatsApp — so every harvest finds a market before it rots.

Our Story

Built by a Student
Who Believed It
Could Be Fixed

HarvestGH was founded by Ibrahim Mohammed Lotsu, an accounting student at Accra Technical University. Ibrahim was not a farmer. He had never grown a crop or visited a village farm. But studying accounting taught him to see systems — and the post-harvest loss problem in Ghana was a broken system at its core.

The problem was not lack of food. It was lack of coordination. Farmers had harvests ready to sell. Buyers had money ready to spend. But the two could not find each other in time before the produce spoiled. No system existed to bridge them — especially not one that worked for elderly, non-literate farmers in rural Ghana.

"Ghana loses 50% of its tomatoes before they reach a buyer. That is not a farming problem. That is a market design problem. And market design problems can be fixed."

HarvestGH was built to be that fix. Simple enough for a 65-year-old farmer who cannot read. Fast enough that a match is made before tomatoes rot. And smart enough that every successful transaction makes the platform stronger for everyone on it.

Ibrahim's strategy starts with FBOs — Farmer Based Organizations — the existing groups of 15–50 farmers already organised by MoFA Ghana. By approaching FBO leaders and registering their entire group at once, HarvestGH can go from zero to 50 farmers in a single afternoon, without asking a single farmer to download an app.

The platform was built entirely in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a Supabase backend — lean, fast, and deployable from a single folder on Vercel. No venture capital. No team. Just a clear idea, a laptop, and the conviction that this problem is solvable.

What HarvestGH Does

Four Things. Done Well.

01
We Connect
We match FBO farmers' produce with bulk buyers — traders, restaurants, schools, and supermarkets — by crop type, region, and quantity.
02
We Notify
Both farmer and buyer receive an SMS the moment a match is confirmed. No app download. Works on any phone, including basic feature phones.
03
We Protect
Buyer payment goes into escrow before produce leaves the farm. Nobody gets cheated. Money is released to the farmer only after delivery is confirmed.
04
We Deliver
We coordinate pickup from farms and delivery to buyers' locations. Farmers don't need to travel to the city. Buyers don't need to chase villages.
