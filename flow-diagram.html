Full Transaction Journey
Every step from first contact to payment received. This is what happens every time a deal is completed on HarvestGH.
1
FBO Leader → HarvestGH
FBO Leader Brings Their Group
The FBO Group Executive contacts Ibrahim. They agree on the agent arrangement — leader earns a commission on every deal their members make.
Ibrahim registers all FBO members in bulk using paper forms. Each farmer chooses: self-managed (controls own listing) or managed (FBO leader handles everything).
2
Farmer → Marketplace
Crop Listing Goes Live
A crop listing appears on the HarvestGH marketplace: crop type, quantity, price, region, pickup location. Visible to all registered buyers.
Example: Ama Kofi · 80 bags Fresh Tomatoes · GH₵120/bag · Kumasi, Ashanti · Available Monday
3
Buyer → HarvestGH
Buyer Registers Their Need
A buyer (trader, restaurant, supermarket, school feeding contractor) registers: crop needed, quantity, region, how often they buy.
Example: Accra Fresh Foods · Needs 60 bags Tomatoes · Weekly · Greater Accra
4
HarvestGH Admin → Match Confirmed
Ibrahim Reviews and Confirms the Match
In the admin panel, Ibrahim sees the suggested match. He reviews that crop type, quantity, and timing align, then clicks "Match & SMS."
The system automatically sends two SMS messages simultaneously — one to the farmer, one to the buyer — with each other's contact details and deal summary.
5
📱 SMS to Farmer & 📱 SMS to Buyer
Both Parties Notified Instantly
Farmer receives: "HarvestGH: A buyer has been found for your 80 bags of Tomato. They will contact you on 0XX XXX XXXX."
Buyer receives: "HarvestGH: Farmer found! 80 bags Tomato · GH₵120/bag · Kumasi. Farmer contact: 0XX XXX XXXX. Questions? Call 0544823484."
6
Buyer → 💰 Payment Escrow → HarvestGH
Buyer Pays into HarvestGH Escrow
Before produce leaves the farm, the buyer pays the full amount via MTN MoMo or Vodafone Cash into HarvestGH's account. The farmer is notified payment is secured.
Why this matters: The farmer has payment guarantee. The buyer has supply guarantee. Neither can cheat the other. This is what keeps both parties on the platform instead of bypassing it.
7
Delivery Partner → Farm → Buyer Location
Delivery Arranged and Completed
A HarvestGH delivery partner picks up from the farm and delivers to the buyer's location. The buyer inspects and confirms receipt.
Delivery fee is separate from the transaction fee and is paid by the buyer. HarvestGH earns a coordination commission on the delivery as well.
8
💰 Escrow Released → Farmer & HarvestGH Fee
✅ Deal Complete — Everyone Gets Paid
Once buyer confirms delivery, HarvestGH releases payment: 98–99% to the farmer, 1–2% fee kept by HarvestGH. The FBO leader also earns their commission.
Farmer earns: GH₵9,408 (98% of GH₵9,600)  |  HarvestGH earns: GH₵192 (2%)  |  FBO Leader earns: commission from HarvestGH's fee
Money & Fee Flow
Exactly how money moves through every HarvestGH transaction — and why the escrow model keeps farmers and buyers on the platform instead of going behind your back.
1
🛒 Buyer Pays Full Amount into HarvestGH Escrow
The buyer pays 100% of the agreed deal value via MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, or bank transfer into HarvestGH's Paystack account. This happens before produce leaves the farm. The farmer is notified by SMS that payment is secured.
🔒 Money is held safely until buyer confirms receipt. Neither party can access it until delivery is confirmed.
2
🚚 Delivery Completes — Buyer Confirms Receipt
The delivery partner drops off produce. The buyer inspects the goods and sends a confirmation to HarvestGH (SMS reply or WhatsApp). Any quality dispute must be raised within 24 hours of delivery.
3
💰 HarvestGH Releases Payment — Split as Follows
98%
Goes to Farmer
Paid via MoMo to farmer's number within 24 hours of confirmation
2%
HarvestGH Platform Fee
HarvestGH's earnings. Part shared with FBO leader as commission.

FBO Partnership Model
How Ibrahim turns one meeting with an FBO leader into 20–50 registered farmers. This is your fastest path to supply at scale.
📞
Step 1 — Ibrahim Contacts FBO Leader
Find FBO leaders through MoFA's district office (ask for the FBO database), community markets, or Facebook groups. Call the Group Executive and request 15 minutes at their next group meeting. Keep the ask simple: "I want to help your farmers find buyers faster."
↓
🗣️
Step 2 — The Group Meeting Pitch (15 minutes)
Open with: "How many of you have lost produce because you couldn't find a buyer in time?" Every hand goes up. Show the platform on your phone. Explain the managed account option. Propose the agent deal to the leader: they earn commission on every deal their members make through HarvestGH.
↓
📝
Step 3 — Bulk Registration on the Spot
Bring printed paper forms. After the meeting, spend 30 minutes collecting: farmer name, phone number, crop type, quantity, price. Every farmer chooses self-managed or managed. FBO leader signs as agent. Ibrahim enters all registrations into HarvestGH that same evening.
↓
🌾
Step 4 — Listings Go Live
All registered farmer crops appear on the marketplace. Ibrahim can also combine multiple farmers' produce into one large listing — "FBO Ashanti: Combined 400 bags Tomatoes from 8 verified farmers" — giving HarvestGH more bargaining power and giving buyers a larger, more reliable supply source than any single farmer could provide.
↓
🤝
Step 5 — First Deal Completes → Trust is Built
When the first farmer in the FBO receives a call saying their produce has a buyer and gets paid through HarvestGH, word spreads through the entire community within days. The FBO leader's commission payment is tangible proof. Every other FBO in the district hears about it — and HarvestGH's reputation grows without advertising.
↓
📈
Step 6 — Repeat and Scale
Each successful FBO partnership leads Ibrahim to the next. Use each deal as a case study: "Ama Kofi from Kumasi FBO sold 80 bags of tomatoes in 3 days through HarvestGH." Take that story to the next FBO meeting. Then the next district. Then approach MoFA to endorse HarvestGH across all their registered FBOs nationally.
