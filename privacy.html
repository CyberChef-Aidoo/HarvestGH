HarvestGH is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect only what we need, use it only for connecting farmers to buyers, and never sell your personal information to anyone.

1. Overview

HarvestGH ("we", "us", "our") operates as a digital agricultural marketplace connecting smallholder farmers to verified buyers across Ghana. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect from farmers, buyers, and visitors who use our website and services.

By registering on HarvestGH or using our platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

This policy applies to all information collected through our website pages, SMS services, and any related services offered by HarvestGH.

2. What Information We Collect

When You Register as a Farmer

  • Full name — to identify you on the platform
  • Phone number — to send you SMS match notifications and contact you when a buyer is found
  • Region — to match you with buyers in or near your area
  • Crop type — to match you with buyers looking for your produce
  • Quantity and price — to display your listing accurately to buyers
  • Pickup location — to help buyers plan logistics
  • Account type — whether you self-manage or want HarvestGH to manage your listing

When You Register as a Buyer

  • Full name — to identify you on the platform
  • Phone number — to send you SMS notifications when a matching farmer is found
  • Business name (optional) — to identify your organisation on the platform
  • Region — to find farmers near you
  • Crop needed and quantity — to match you with appropriate farmers

When You Contact Us

  • Your name, phone number, and message content from our contact form

Technical Information

  • We do not collect IP addresses, cookies, or tracking data beyond what Netlify (our hosting provider) automatically logs for security purposes
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics that identify individual users

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes only:

  • Matching: Comparing farmer listings and buyer requests by crop type, region, and quantity to find suitable connections
  • SMS Notifications: Sending you a text message when a match has been found for your listing or buyer request
  • Platform Management: Our admin team reviews farmer and buyer registrations to verify listings and manage the matching process manually
  • Support: Responding to questions and resolving issues you raise with us
  • Broadcast Messages: Sending seasonal announcements or platform updates to registered users (you can opt out by contacting us)
  • Platform Improvement: Using aggregated, anonymised data (not linked to individuals) to understand which crops and regions have the most activity

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose not listed above.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

HarvestGH shares your information in limited, specific circumstances:

With Matched Parties

When a match is confirmed between a farmer and a buyer, we share the farmer's phone number with the buyer and the buyer's phone number with the farmer. This is the core purpose of the platform — you agree to this when you register.

With Service Providers

  • Supabase — our database provider, based in the United States. Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. Supabase is compliant with industry security standards.
  • Hubtel — our SMS provider, based in Ghana. Your phone number is shared with Hubtel solely to deliver SMS notifications.
  • Netlify — our website hosting provider. Netlify handles website delivery but does not have access to your registered data.

With NGOs, Government, or Research Partners

We may share anonymised, aggregated statistics (e.g. "450 tomato farmers registered in Ashanti Region") with partners such as MoFA Ghana, NGOs, or researchers. This data does not identify any individual person.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by Ghanaian law, court order, or legitimate legal process.

We never sell your personal data to any third party for any reason, including advertising.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secure Supabase database with the following protections:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) ensures your data is only accessible by authorised parties
  • Admin access requires authenticated login through Supabase Auth
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
  • Supabase databases are encrypted at rest

We retain your registration data for as long as your account is active on HarvestGH. If you request deletion of your account and data, we will remove your information within 14 days.

We cannot guarantee absolute security — no digital system can. However, we take reasonable and industry-standard precautions to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

As a registered user of HarvestGH, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Ask us to delete your account and all associated personal data
  • Opt-out of SMS: Ask us to stop sending you SMS messages (note: this will prevent you from receiving match notifications)
  • Portability: Request your data in a readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10 below. We will respond within 14 days.

7. SMS Communications

By providing your phone number when registering, you consent to receiving SMS messages from HarvestGH. These messages include:

  • Match notifications — when a buyer or farmer is found for you
  • Listing confirmations — when your listing is posted
  • Seasonal announcements — about upcoming crop seasons or platform updates (infrequent)

SMS messages are sent through Hubtel, a licensed Ghanaian SMS provider. Standard network charges from your mobile carrier may apply to received SMS messages depending on your plan.

To opt out of SMS communications, send "STOP" in reply to any HarvestGH SMS, or contact us directly. Note that opting out of SMS means you will not receive match notifications and will need to check your listing status manually at farmer-portal.html.

8. Children and Minors

HarvestGH is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has registered on our platform, please contact us and we will promptly remove that account.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our platform evolves. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

If you are a registered user, we will notify you of major privacy policy changes via SMS. Continued use of HarvestGH after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
