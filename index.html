How It Works

Simple Enough for Any Phone.
Powerful Enough for Any Farm.

🇬🇭 Built for Ghana
🤝
1. FBO Leader Partners With HarvestGH
Ibrahim meets with the FBO group leader. The leader registers their entire member group in one session — 15 to 50 farmers at once.
📦
2. Produce Listed on the Marketplace
HarvestGH uploads the FBO's crops with photos, prices, and quantities. Buyers across Ghana can immediately see and order.
💰
3. Buyer Orders and Pays Securely
A buyer places an order and pays via Paystack or MoMo. The money is held in escrow until delivery is confirmed.
🚚
4. Delivery Arranged — Everyone Gets Paid
Produce is picked up from the farm and delivered. When the buyer confirms receipt, the farmer is paid. HarvestGH earns a small 1–2% platform fee.
