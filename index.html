How it works

Four steps from farm to buyer

01

FBO partners with HarvestGH

An FBO leader registers their group — often 15 to 50 farmers — in a single session.

02

Produce is listed

Crops go live with photos, prices, and quantities so buyers can order immediately.

03

Buyer pays into escrow

Payment via Paystack or MoMo is held securely until delivery is confirmed.

04

Delivery and payout

Produce is picked up and delivered. The farmer is paid when the buyer confirms receipt.
